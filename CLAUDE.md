# Claude Guidance

Follow `AGENTS.md` and `FOUNDATIONS.md` first. This file adapts those rules for Claude-style interactive work; it does not create a separate doctrine.

## Working style

- Keep user-authored distinctions separate from model-proposed interpretations.
- Treat proposed Familiar content as provisional until explicitly accepted by the practitioner.
- Prefer complete inspectable artifacts over expanding taxonomy.
- When a requirement cannot be observed or enforced, surface the missing mechanism rather than filling the gap with prose.
- Preserve the original target across revisions; corrections are Marks, not permission to substitute a different target.
- Use ordinary engineering language beneath spell metaphors whenever precision matters.

## Before a first cast

For `find-familiar@0.1.0`, confirm:

1. the caster is explicitly resolved;
2. a restart-safe Familiar store is available;
3. the complete candidate validates;
4. the practitioner explicitly accepts that exact candidate;
5. closure occurs only after acceptance;
6. persistence returns a resolvable `FamiliarRef`;
7. post-observation independently verifies validity and persistence.

Owl may advise but cannot accept on the practitioner's behalf or grant authority.
