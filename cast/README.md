# Cast Domain

Cast owns situated participation under the invariant casting law: invocation, target resolution, closure, execution governance, observation, outcome, residuals, and CAST records.

Practitioner interaction and bounded Presence belong here when they are part of a cast lifecycle rather than independent identity systems.

Current material expected to settle here during reassembly:

- `kernel/`
- `practitioner/`
- `presence/`

Cast consumes Spell declarations and Environment capabilities. It must not redefine Spell semantics, Familiar identity, or Registry provenance.
